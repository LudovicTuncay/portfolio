The portfolio codebase is intentionally simple in architecture: static Astro pages for the shell, small Svelte islands where interaction is useful, and content collections for structured publishing. The goal is to keep the site pleasant to edit while still allowing custom layouts such as the bento research grid and the photography presentation system.

The project is also where I can treat design and writing as part of the work, not just as packaging around it. That makes it a useful code project in its own right rather than a passive repository.

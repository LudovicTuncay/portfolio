This repository is the main working codebase behind several ongoing research directions in self-supervised audio representation learning. It is designed around repeatable experiments rather than one-off scripts: configurations stay explicit, model families share conventions, and evaluation can be run against the same benchmark suite.

The project currently covers Audio-JEPA, RQA-JEPA, BEST-RQ, and BEST-RQ-2 style runs. It also serves as the bridge between paper ideas and benchmark results, which keeps implementation details close to the claims made in the papers.

## What is inside

- Shared training and evaluation workflows.
- Configuration-driven experimentation with Hydra.
- A single home for model variants and benchmark comparisons.
